What is Deal Flow CRM?

Deal Flow CRM is an AI-powered sales CRM for small and growing sales teams around the world. It tells each salesperson who to call today, keeps leads, WhatsApp chats, emails, calls and quotes in one place, and includes an AI calling agent that answers missed calls and calls lead lists in 40+ languages and local accents, then books the meeting. It is free for 5 leads, and paid plans start at $19 per user a month.

Questions, answered.

What is Deal Flow CRM?

A CRM for sales teams that tells each person who to call today, keeps every lead, chat and quote in one place, and comes with an AI calling agent that answers and makes calls for you.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every feature, the AI included, for up to 5 leads and 5 deals. No card needed.

How does the AI calling agent work?

It runs on a Windows laptop with our free Call Box app, connected to your own phone by Bluetooth, and uses your Google Gemini key for its voice. It answers calls you miss and calls your lists, in the caller's language, then saves the recording, transcript and summary on the lead.

Which languages does the AI speak?

More than 40 languages, each in a local accent: English, Spanish, Hindi and Hinglish among them. It keeps to the caller's language.

How much does it cost?

Basic is $19 (₹1,000 in India) and Pro is $49 (₹2,000 in India) per seat a month, paid yearly. AI calling, WhatsApp and Ask AI are in Pro.

Can I pay online?

Yes. Choose your plan and seats on the pricing page, sign in or create your account, and pay online by card (and UPI or net banking in India).

Can I import my leads?

Yes, from Excel, CSV, or exports of Zoho, HubSpot, Odoo, Pipedrive and Salesforce.

Where is my data stored?

On Google Cloud, in the Mumbai region, encrypted in transit and at rest. Call recordings can go to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.
