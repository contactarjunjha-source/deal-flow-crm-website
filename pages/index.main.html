What is Deal Flow CRM?

Deal Flow CRM is an AI-powered sales CRM, built in India, for small and growing sales teams. It tells each salesperson who to call today, keeps leads, WhatsApp chats, emails, calls and GST quotes in one place, and includes an AI calling agent that answers missed calls and calls lead lists in 40+ languages, including Hindi and Hinglish, then books the meeting. It is free for 5 leads, and paid plans start at ₹1,000 per user a month.

Questions, answered.

What is Deal Flow CRM?

A CRM for sales teams that tells each person who to call today, keeps every lead, chat and quote in one place, and comes with an AI calling agent that answers and makes calls for you.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every feature, the AI included, for up to 5 leads and 5 deals. No card needed.

How does the AI calling agent work?

It runs on a Windows laptop with our free Call Box app, connected to your own phone by Bluetooth, and uses your Google Gemini key for its voice. It answers calls you miss and calls your lists, in the caller's language, then saves the recording, transcript and summary on the lead.

Which languages does the AI speak?

More than 40 languages, in India and abroad, including Hindi, Hinglish and English with an Indian voice.

How much does it cost?

Basic is ₹1,000 and Pro is ₹2,000 per seat a month, paid yearly, GST included. AI calling, WhatsApp and Ask AI are in Pro.

Can I pay online?

Yes. Choose your plan and seats on the pricing page, sign in or create your account, and pay by UPI, card or net banking.

Can I import my leads?

Yes, from Excel, CSV, or exports of Zoho, HubSpot, Odoo, Pipedrive and Salesforce.

Where is my data stored?

On Google Cloud in Mumbai, India. Call recordings can go to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.
