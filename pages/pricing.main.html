Billing, answered.

How do I pay?

Through Cashfree, by UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking. You pay for a year at a time, GST included.

Can I pay before creating an account?

Your payment is linked to your workspace, so you sign in or sign up first. It takes a minute, and the payment opens right after.

Does it renew automatically?

No. We remind you 14, 7 and 1 day before your year ends. Renewal opens 30 days before.

What if I don't renew?

Three days after the year ends you move to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.

Can I add people mid-year?

Yes. Extra seats and upgrades to Pro are charged only for the days left in your year.

Do you give refunds?

For billing errors, like a duplicate charge or a plan that never activated. See our refund policy for details.
