Data Handling

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. What we store

We store the data you submit to Deal Flow CRM, including account details, deal records, notes, documents and billing information. We also store limited technical data needed to operate the service.

2. Where data is stored

Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure. We choose providers with strong security practices and keep data in regions appropriate to our customers.

3. Access controls

Access to your data is restricted to your team based on roles and permissions. Our staff can access data only when needed for support or operations, and only under documented procedures.

4. Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and at rest using industry standard encryption. We protect credentials with secure hashing.

5. Backups

We perform regular backups to protect against data loss. Backups are encrypted and retained on a rolling schedule.

6. Subprocessors

We use a small set of trusted subprocessors for hosting, payments, authentication and email. Each is bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations. A current list is available on request.

7. Retention

We retain data while your account is active. After cancellation or deletion request, we remove data from active systems within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law. See Data Deletion.

8. Security incidents

If we become aware of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you without undue delay and take steps to mitigate the impact.
