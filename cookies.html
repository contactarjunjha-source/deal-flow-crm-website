Cookies

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit.

2. Types we use

We use essential cookies required for the service to function, such as authentication and security. We may use preference cookies to remember your settings. We also use the Meta Pixel to measure our own advertising.

3. Third party cookies

Our service providers may set cookies needed to provide their services, such as payment processing or authentication. These are governed by their own policies.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) sets cookies through the Meta Pixel to tell us whether our ads lead to visits, sign-ups and subscriptions, and to show our ads to the right people. It receives page visits with your browser and device details; it does not receive your CRM data. You can block it with your browser's third-party cookie settings or an ad blocker, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

4. How to control cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the service.

5. Changes

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page.
